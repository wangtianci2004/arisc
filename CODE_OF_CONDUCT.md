# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ARISC a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving constructive
feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the
community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling,
insults, harassment, publishing private information without permission, and
other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Report unacceptable behavior privately through the security contact
or maintainer contact published by the repository owner. Maintainers will
review reports promptly, respect the reporter's privacy, and take corrective
action proportionate to the circumstances.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
